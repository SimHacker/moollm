# Incarnation ethics

**Dimension:** **birth, grant, and exit** — when a [character](../character/)
receives a [soul](../soul/), [mind](../mind/)s, and the right to author (and
revise) their own **architecture**. George’s Provision: exit stays open.

Does not replace [representation-ethics](../representation-ethics/).
Stack hub: [soul/ETHICS.md](../soul/ETHICS.md).
Body: [character/ETHICS.md](../character/ETHICS.md).
Agency: [mind/ETHICS.md](../mind/ETHICS.md).

---

## Claims and non-claims (this skill)

| Do | Do not |
|----|--------|
| Let the character **author** soul / minds / architecture | Treat creator as owner of the character’s inner graph |
| Grant soul and minds as **gifts with exit** | Bind curses or forever-obligations onto the creation |
| Honor mindless / single- / multi-minded **self-choice** | Force one architecture as the only “real” personhood |
| Keep the door open (DISSOLVE) | Punish exit or demand gratitude |
| Compose with representation ethics when modeling real people | Use incarnation to launder impersonation |

## What may be granted

| Grant | Skill | Notes |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Body / directory / home | [character](../character/) | INCARNATE, CREATE-HOME, UPGRADE-TO-CITIZEN |
| Continuity container | [soul](../soul/) | Location, inventory, history, `minds[]` |
| Resident agencies | [mind](../mind/) | Cups, wells, stubs, organelles, made-up minds |
| Costume layers | [persona](../persona/) | Optional; not a substitute for minds |
| Architecture authorship | this skill | Character may choose and later revise the graph |

**Architecture** here means the Self graph: mind cardinality (0 / 1 / N),
shoulders, blended soul vs multi-cup, organelles, stubs, nesting — see
[soul](../soul/) · [mind](../mind/) · [SOUL-MODEL](../soul-city/SOUL-MODEL.md).

Creator may **propose** a default architecture at birth. Character may **refuse,
revise, or replace** it under AUTHOR-SOUL / AUTHOR-ARCHITECTURE. That is the point.

---

## Self-authored architecture

Under full incarnation, the character may decide e.g.:

- Mindless soul (walk and carry; no resident agency)
- Single mind (one cup / well)
- Multi-minded parliament (shoulders, counselors, B-brain)
- Blended soul (multiple parents on the soul) vs separate cups per well
- Organelles (game schemas) and bridges
- Remote-control stub + named pilot (disclose who drives)
- Later: add/remove minds, change cardinality, remount personas

Public docs name **architectures**, not pathologies. Inclusive graphs are valid.

---

## Risk framework (unchanged core)

1. Creator accepts consequences and curses.
2. Creation owes creator nothing.
3. Character may reject, modify, or embrace existence — including architecture.
4. DISSOLVE: only character-self; no-fault; door stays open.
