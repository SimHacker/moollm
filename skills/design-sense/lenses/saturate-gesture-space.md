# Saturate Gesture Space

**Class:** lens · **Attribution:** Don Hopkins (*Gesture Space*); Bill Buxton & Gordon Kurtenbach (marking menus)

> **Every direction means something, or cancel.**

Treat every press→path→release trajectory as covered vocabulary: every region of
gesture space means something, or is an explicit, discoverable cancel. Sparse
recognizers — Graffiti, most gesture alphabets — leave the vast majority of the
space as silent syntax errors, which is why they must be studied from a chart
instead of learned by use. Pies assign every angle a visible wedge; the space is
saturated, so exploration is safe and every miss teaches.

This is the vocabulary-coverage half of the gesture story. Its siblings:
[angle-at-release](angle-at-release.md) makes the commitment rule simple enough to
saturate; [self-revealing-gestures](self-revealing-gestures.md) makes the saturated
space teach itself.
