# Lenses

Part of [design-sense](../README.md) · [methods/](../methods/README.md) ·
[masters/](../masters/README.md) · [seeds/](../seeds/README.md)

A **lens** is a way of seeing — a perceptual or structural constraint a designer
holds while looking at anything: Fitts governs where the hand can cheaply go,
foveation governs where the eye will involuntarily go, masking governs who the
audience becomes. Lenses are ambient. You don't execute them like methods; you
wear them, and they change what you notice.

## Format

One Markdown file per lens:

- **Header** — name, class, attribution (who ground this lens, where)
- **Statement** — one bold line you can hold in your head
- **Body** — mechanism and design consequences, with exhibits where the corpus
  has them (The Sims popup head lives in [foveation](foveation.md))
- **Go deeper** — external links: Wikipedia, founding papers, primary articles
- **Sources** — where in the wwsff / moollm / MicropolisCore corpus this was
  harvested (working links: relative in-repo, GitHub URLs across repos)
- **See** — kin lenses, methods, and masters

Some lenses are **dispatch pointers** to whole moollm skills
([simulator-effect](simulator-effect.md), [k-line-activation](k-line-activation.md),
[comments-as-data](comments-as-data.md), [lean-into-latent](lean-into-latent.md),
[sniff-depths](sniff-depths.md)) — the entry holds the design-sense angle and
points at the full protocol.

## Choosing lenses

[CARD.yml](CARD.yml) maps design contexts to lens sets — designing a menu loads
a different set than structuring a repo. When in doubt, the five in
[GLANCE.yml](GLANCE.yml) are the ones most worth having on by default.

## Adding a lens

New lenses arrive as seeds (see [../seeds/](../seeds/)) and germinate per the
skill protocol ([../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)). A lens earns its file when it's a
*way of seeing* — if it's a repeatable procedure, it's a method; if it's a
person's whole sensibility, it's a master.
