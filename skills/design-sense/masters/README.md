# Masters

Part of [design-sense](../README.md) · [lenses/](../lenses/README.md) ·
[methods/](../methods/README.md) · [seeds/](../seeds/README.md)

A **master** file is a whole design sensibility compressed to one page — a head
you can borrow. Where a [lens](../lenses/README.md) is one way of seeing and a
[method](../methods/README.md) is one way of working, a master is the person the
lenses and methods were ground from, with the connective tissue intact.

## Format

- **Head** — the two-or-three-idea core of the sensibility
- **Votes** — positive imperatives: what this head tells you to *do*, bulletized
  and linked to the plugins and sources that cash each one
- **Vetoes** — the lines this head won't let you cross
- **Plugins attributed** — the lenses and methods this master grounds
- **Sources** — primary works and corpus locations, all working links

Votes and vetoes are the operating interface: load a master before a design
session and the votes push, the vetoes block. Load several for an adversarial
committee (moollm `skills/adversarial-committee/`) — [Gabriel](richard-gabriel.md)
vs [Ungar](david-ungar.md) on what "simple" means is a genuinely productive
fight.

## Choosing a head

[CARD.yml](CARD.yml) dispatches by problem: pointing and menus, simulation,
emergence, live systems, learning, worlds and communities, explanation,
attention, assistants, philosophy.

## Admission

Masters are grounded in the corpus (wwsff characters, moollm characters,
lloooomm roster at `temp/lloooomm/00-Characters/`). One person, one file. The
bar: their sensibility must vote and veto — a portfolio isn't a head.
