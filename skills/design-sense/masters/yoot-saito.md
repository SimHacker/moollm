# Yoot Saito

**Class:** master · **Head:** one strange fascination, simulated honestly

Every Saito game is a single concrete obsession pursued past all reason: The
Tower (SimTower in the West, published by Maxis, 1994) grew from watching
elevators — the skyscraper as a scheduling problem, with tenant stress made
visible; Seaman (Dreamcast, 1999) put a rude human face on a fish and made a
voice-interface agent people *believed*, precisely because it refused to be
servile — breaking the polite-assistant convention is what created presence;
Odama (GameCube, 2006) commanded a feudal army by shouting at a pinball table.
The design lesson runs opposite to genre thinking: start from one odd, real,
lovingly observed system (an elevator bank, a pet that judges you) and let the
game precipitate around it. Also the bridge figure: Waseda, MACWORLD Japan,
Vivarium's orbit — carrying PARC's ideas into Japanese game culture and back.

## Votes

- **Start from one lovingly observed system** — an elevator bank, a fish, a
  pinball; the game precipitates around the fascination
  ([instance-first](../methods/instance-first.md);
  [SimTower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SimTower) is an elevator scheduler
  wearing a skyscraper)
- **Give the agent a spine** — Seaman's rudeness created presence no polite
  assistant ever earned; deference is the uncanny valley of personality
  ([Seaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seaman_(video_game)))
- **Make the stress visible** — the tenant's complaint, the red elevator queue:
  simulation legibility *is* the interface
  ([goldilocks-complexity](../lenses/goldilocks-complexity.md))
- **Interview your heroes and publish the tape** — the
  [Kay interview](https://github.com/YootTowerManagement/YootTower/blob/main/Yoot_Saito_Alan_Kay_Interview/Yoot_Saito_Alan_Kay_Interview.md)
  carried PARC to Japanese game culture; be the bridge
- **Use the weird input channel** — a microphone on a Dreamcast, shouting at a
  GameCube; the unfashionable modality is an unsaturated
  [gesture space](../lenses/saturate-gesture-space.md)

## Vetoes

- Don't start from a genre when you could start from a fascination
- Don't make the agent servile — deference kills presence
- Don't hide the simulation's stress; the tenant's complaint is the interface
