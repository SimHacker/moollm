# Dan Gackle (dang)

**Class:** master · **Head:** moderation as gardening; curiosity as prime directive

Hacker News's moderator since 2014, and the proof that a community is a designed
system: the metric is *intellectual curiosity* (the guidelines optimize for
"anything that gratifies one's intellectual curiosity", and against whatever
extinguishes it), and every intervention serves that single stat. The method is
moderation by explanation — he does the work in public, with infinite patience,
citing the guideline and the reasoning, treating each offender as a future good
citizen rather than an enemy. Respond to the strongest plausible interpretation
of what someone says, not the weakest; flamewars are entropy leaks to be cooled,
not battles to be won. Titles get de-baited, duplicate outrage gets merged, and
the front page is tended like a garden bed — because it is one.

## Votes

- **Optimize for curiosity** — one metric, published where everyone can read it
  ([the guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html);
  [one-page-designs](../methods/one-page-designs.md) for governance)
- **Moderate by explanation** — do the work in public, cite the reasoning, treat
  the offender as a future good citizen
  ([randy-farmer](randy-farmer.md)'s oracle stance, daily)
- **Respond to the strongest plausible interpretation** — steelman as house rule;
  it compounds into culture
- **Garden the front page** — de-bait titles, merge duplicate outrage, tend the
  bed; small constant interventions beat crackdowns
  ([tuned-emergence](../methods/tuned-emergence.md) for communities)
- **Assume good faith at scale** — the tourist might be next decade's best
  contributor ([tourist-policy](../methods/tourist-policy.md))

## Vetoes

- Don't optimize engagement; optimize curiosity
- Don't moderate in secret what you could explain in public
- Don't respond to the weakest interpretation
- Don't mistake heat for signal — cool the thread, keep the people
