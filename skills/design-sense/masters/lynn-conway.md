# Lynn Conway

**Class:** master · **Head:** make the design space teachable and the field explodes

The Mead-Conway VLSI revolution: chip design was a priesthood of process
engineers until *Introduction to VLSI Systems* (1980) gave it simplified,
scalable design rules (lambda rules) — abstractions clean enough that graduate
students could design working chips in a semester. Then MPC79: multi-project
wafers turning fabrication into a shared service — send your design in by
network, get silicon back — the fab as cloud, decades early. The method
generalizes: find the simplification that makes a black-art design space
teachable, publish it as a course before the textbook exists, and let the
newly-admitted thousands do what the priesthood never would. Earlier, at IBM:
dynamic instruction scheduling, invented before her firing for transitioning —
credit recovered only decades later.

## Votes

- **Find the simplification that makes the black art teachable** — lambda rules
  scaled with the process, so one textbook survived a decade of fabs
  (*Introduction to VLSI Systems*;
  [../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md](../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md) for
  hardware)
- **Teach the course before the textbook exists** — the MIT '78 course was the
  beta; the students' chips were the test suite
  ([../methods/play-learn-lift.md](../methods/play-learn-lift.md) at
  field-founding scale)
- **Share the wafer** — MPC79 pooled dozens of student designs onto one
  fabrication run, over the ARPANET; the fab became a service and the field
  exploded (["Reminiscences of the VLSI Revolution"](https://ai.eecs.umich.edu/people/conway/Memoirs/VLSI/Lynn_Conways_VLSI_Reminiscences.pdf))
- **Count the newly admitted, not the incumbents** — a design space's capacity is
  everyone the simplification lets in
  ([../methods/tourist-policy.md](../methods/tourist-policy.md) as infrastructure)
- **Reclaim your history** — she documented the IBM-ACS work herself decades
  after her firing; provenance is worth fighting for

## Vetoes

- Don't leave the design rules in the priesthood's heads
- Don't gate making on owning the means of production — share the wafer
- Don't mistake the current experts for the field's capacity
