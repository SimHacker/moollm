# Seymour Papert

**Class:** master · **Head:** constructionism; microworlds; objects to think with
**Dispatch:** whole skill at moollm `skills/constructionism/` — load it; this entry is the design-sense pointer.

Learning is building inspectable things in public — constructionism, learning by
making shareable artifacts. Microworlds: bounded, honest little universes
(Mathland, the turtle) where the laws are learnable by living in them. The turtle
is body-syntonic — you debug by walking the program with your own body — and it's
the type specimen of an *object to think with*. Debugging is not failure recovery;
debugging IS the learning. Low floor, high ceiling is his standard for every
learning tool ([low-floor-no-ceiling](../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md) traces
the lineage through Resnick's wide walls to Snap!'s no ceiling).

## Votes

- **Let them build shareable things** — knowledge is constructed by making an
  artifact someone else can inspect (constructionism: moollm
  `skills/constructionism/`, [*Mindstorms*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindstorms_(book)))
- **Make the bug the curriculum** — debugging is the most honest form of thinking
  about thinking ([play-learn-lift](../methods/play-learn-lift.md))
- **Build microworlds with honest laws** — small enough to live in, true enough to
  trust ([goldilocks-complexity](../lenses/goldilocks-complexity.md))
- **Give them objects to think with** — the turtle is body-syntonic: walk the
  program with your own body before writing it
  ([everything-is-concrete](../methods/everything-is-concrete.md))
- **Keep the floor low and the ceiling out of reach** —
  ([low-floor-no-ceiling](../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md); his heirs:
  [mitch-resnick](mitch-resnick.md), [brian-harvey](brian-harvey.md),
  [cynthia-solomon](cynthia-solomon.md))
- **Make Mathland** — the way to learn French is to live in France; build the
  country where the knowledge is the native language

## Vetoes

- Don't teach about; let them build in
- Don't treat bugs as shame — the bug is the curriculum
- Don't build a microworld whose laws lie for convenience
- Don't confuse instruction (pouring in) with construction (building out)
