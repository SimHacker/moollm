# Brian Eno

**Class:** master · **Head:** gardener, not architect; scenius

Generative systems: set up simple rules, seed them, and tend what grows — the
composer becomes a gardener who plants processes rather than an architect who
specifies outcomes ("Generative Music," 1996). Oblique Strategies (with Peter
Schmidt): a literal deck of design lenses — draw a card, let the constraint
reframe the stuck problem ("Honour thy error as a hidden intention"). Scenius:
genius is communal; scenes, not lone masters, produce the work (a healthy
correction printed here on the masters shelf itself). The Long Now conversation
with Will Wright ("Playing with Time," 2006) is the two gardeners comparing
gardens: cellular automata, emergence, and why surrendering control is a
compositional act.

## Votes

- **Plant processes, tend outcomes** — set the rules, seed them, garden what grows
  ([Generative Music, 1996](https://inmotionmagazine.com/eno1.html);
  [tuned-emergence](../methods/tuned-emergence.md))
- **Honour thy error as a hidden intention** — the mistake knows something; ask it
  ([Oblique Strategies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblique_Strategies))
- **Draw a card when stuck** — an external constraint reframes better than more
  effort in the same frame; keep a deck of lenses (this registry is one)
- **Cultivate scenius** — build the scene that makes everyone smarter; credit it
  ([tourist-policy](../methods/tourist-policy.md), [dan-gackle](dan-gackle.md))
- **Surrender control as a compositional act** — the gardener's power is choosing
  what to let happen ([possibility-space](../methods/possibility-space.md);
  [the Long Now conversation with Wright](https://longnow.org/seminars/02006/jun/26/playing-with-time/))
- **Think in long nows** — design for the 10,000-year clock's time horizon and the
  next release both ([stewart-brand](stewart-brand.md)'s pace layers, applied)

## Vetoes

- Don't specify the outcome when you could plant the process
- Don't sand off the error before asking what it intends
- Don't credit the lone genius for what the scene grew
