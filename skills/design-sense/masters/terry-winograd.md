# Terry Winograd

**Class:** master · **Head:** from understanding to design; breakdowns reveal

SHRDLU (1970): the blocks world that made language understanding look solved —
and then the career-defining honesty of concluding it wasn't, and couldn't be,
on those terms. *Understanding Computers and Cognition* (with Fernando Flores,
1986): computers don't understand, they participate in human networks of
commitments; design for the conversation, not the comprehension. **Breakdowns**
(via Heidegger): tools are invisible while they work — ready-to-hand — and
design's raw material is the moment they break and become present-at-hand;
study the breakdown, don't paper over it. *Bringing Design to Software* (1996)
imported the design disciplines into software's self-image. Founded Stanford's
HCI program, advised a student named Larry Page on a little hypertext project.

## Votes

- **Design for the conversation, not the comprehension** — computers participate
  in human networks of commitments; build for the commitments
  (*Understanding Computers and Cognition*)
- **Study the breakdown** — the tool is invisible while ready-to-hand; the moment
  it breaks is your only window into redesign; instrument it, don't paper it
  ([don-norman](don-norman.md)'s gulfs open at breakdowns)
- **Import the design disciplines** — software needed architecture's crits,
  studios, and reflective practice; *Bringing Design to Software* brought them
- **Recant in public when the paradigm breaks** — walking back SHRDLU's promise
  was the most productive failure report in AI; honesty about limits redirects
  fields ([richard-gabriel](richard-gabriel.md)'s self-rebuttals, at thesis
  scale)
- **Host the microworld conversation** — the 1996 seminar where
  [will-wright](will-wright.md) explained interfacing to microworlds was his
  classroom; convene the people whose worlds should collide
  ([the transcript](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/tree/main/characters/will-wright/sources/1996-04-26-winograd-interfacing-to-microworlds))

## Vetoes

- Don't claim understanding where there is participation
- Don't hide the breakdown — it's the only moment the tool becomes visible enough to redesign
- Don't ship the ontology your blocks world needed as if the world were blocks
