# Ken Kahn

**Class:** master · **Head:** programming as an animated world

ToonTalk (1995): not a visual programming language but a programming *world* —
computation as a cartoon city where birds carry messages to their nests
(message-passing), trucks drive off to spawn workers (process creation), bombs
explode houses (termination), and Dusty the vacuum erases (garbage collection).
The radical move: the semantics underneath is concurrent constraint logic
programming — research-grade, no ceiling — but every abstraction wears a body a
five-year-old can grab. Programs are built by demonstration inside the world,
then generalized by removing detail (wave the magic wand, the specific becomes
the variable). It's the whole design-sense stack in one artifact: concrete first,
abstraction by erosion, deep semantics in a costume.

## Votes

- **Give every abstraction a body** — birds carry messages, trucks spawn
  processes, Dusty vacuums the garbage; the semantics wears a costume a
  five-year-old can grab ([ToonTalk](http://www.toontalk.com/);
  [../methods/everything-is-concrete.md](../methods/everything-is-concrete.md))
- **Pick a research-grade model underneath** — concurrent constraint logic
  programming under the cartoons: no ceiling hiding under the low floor
  ([../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md](../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md))
- **Generalize by erasure** — demonstrate with the specific, then wave the wand
  and remove detail; the variable is what's left when the example fades
  ([../methods/instance-first.md](../methods/instance-first.md) as a literal
  gesture; [allen-cypher](allen-cypher.md)'s inference, made manual and visible)
- **Program inside the world, not about it** — you build the robot's plan by
  doing the job once yourself, in the house, with your hands
- **Let the metaphor be load-bearing** — the city isn't decoration; every
  ToonTalk metaphor maps one-to-one onto the computation it dresses
  ([../lenses/mental-model-compiler.md](../lenses/mental-model-compiler.md) —
  the metaphor is the compile target)

## Vetoes

- Don't show the child a syntax when the semantics could wear a body
- Don't dumb down the computation model — dress it up instead
- Don't ask for the abstraction — take an example and erase the specifics
