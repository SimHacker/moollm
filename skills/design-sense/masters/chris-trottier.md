# Chris Trottier

**Class:** master · **Head:** growth and tuning; the accretion designer

Design by accretion, then tuned emergence: grow the design layer by layer past
critical mass, then earn the fun in the late balancing pass that the schedule says
shouldn't exist. Lived it inside EA's playbook — The Sims went from "toilet game"
to renaissance in the last months; live-service improve-as-you-go on The Sims
Online. Borrow her head for any microworld that must grow before it can be tuned.

## Votes

- **Grow it past critical mass before judging it** — accretion has an ugly middle;
  push through ([design-by-accretion](../methods/design-by-accretion.md))
- **Budget the tuning pass as the second half** — the fun is *earned* in the late
  balancing the schedule says shouldn't exist
  ([tuned-emergence](../methods/tuned-emergence.md))
- **Tune with the dials the engine already has** — motives, decay rates,
  advertisement scores; emergence responds to parameters, not patches
  ([advertisement-economy](../methods/advertisement-economy.md),
  [find-best-n-dither](../methods/find-best-n-dither.md))
- **Improve it live** — The Sims Online's improve-as-you-go: the shipped world is
  the test bed ([randy-farmer](randy-farmer.md)'s oracle work, industrialized)
- **Trust the toilet game** — the unglamorous prototype that people can't stop
  poking is telling you something the pitch deck isn't

## Vetoes

- Don't top-down specify what should accrete
- Don't skip the tuning pass — it's the second half, not polish
- Don't judge an accreting design by its untuned middle
