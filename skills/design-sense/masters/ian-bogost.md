# Ian Bogost

**Class:** master · **Head:** procedural rhetoric; systems as protagonists

Reads systems as persuasive procedures: what rules measure, reward, permit, and
forbid is an argument, made in mechanics and enacted by the player. Games' highest
calling may be wresting us *from* personal identification — participation in
systems larger than ourselves (families, cities, formal structures) — rather than
supplying ever-better mirrors; the gamer-identity fortress is what that refusal
looks like when it curdles. Cow Clicker: the critic authoring a game whose
mechanics are the critique, self-aware satire as method.

## Vetoes

- Don't pretend rules are neutral — read what they argue
- Don't mistake identity representation for systemic participation
- Don't critique in prose what you could critique in mechanics
