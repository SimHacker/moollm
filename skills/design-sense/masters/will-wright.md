# Will Wright

**Class:** master · **Head:** simulation-game design; toys, not games

Possibility space over storyline; the player is the storyteller. Failure is
entertainment — a fire, a flood, a ruined wedding is content, not error — and it
must stay *legible* (Goldilocks complexity: SimEarth too opaque, SimAnt too simple,
SimCity 2000 just right). The simulator effect: games run on two computers, the
tame one on the desk and the wild one in the player's head — implication beats
simulation, so don't implement what the imagination renders better. Intelligence
lives in the environment: objects advertise scored offers, agents choose by needs,
and the pick dithers among the top N on purpose. The hobby model: extensible
user-created content over movie-model lock-in.

## Votes

- **Build a [possibility space](../methods/possibility-space.md) and hand the player
  the keys** — the design is the landscape, not the tour
- **Put the player's imagination on the payroll** — imply, don't render; the wild
  computer in their head out-simulates yours ([simulator-effect](../lenses/simulator-effect.md))
- **Make failure funny, legible, and worth photographing** — the fire is content
  ([failure-as-entertainment](../methods/failure-as-entertainment.md),
  [goldilocks-complexity](../lenses/goldilocks-complexity.md))
- **Put the intelligence in the environment** — objects advertise scored offers,
  agents stay simple ([advertisement-economy](../methods/advertisement-economy.md))
- **Dither among the best N** — perfection reads as robotic; near-best reads as alive
  ([find-best-n-dither](../methods/find-best-n-dither.md))
- **Ship the hobby, not the movie** — player-created content compounds; authored
  content depreciates ([player-as-storyteller](../methods/player-as-storyteller.md))
- **Design the mental model first** — the game compiles into the player's head, so
  check what it compiles *to* ([mental-model-compiler](../lenses/mental-model-compiler.md))
- **Plunder other disciplines** — Christopher Alexander, Montessori toys, cellular
  automata; the good ideas are mostly outside games
  ([one-page](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1013561/(304)-Design-Plunder) — GDC "Design Plunder")

## Vetoes

- Don't script what can emerge
- Don't over-specify what implication supplies for free
- Don't centralize behavior that can live in drop-in objects
- Don't let failure become opaque — if the player can't tell why, the complexity is wrong
