# Mitch Resnick

**Class:** master · **Head:** wide walls; lifelong kindergarten

Added the third axis to Papert's floor and ceiling: **wide walls** — a tool must
support many kinds of projects for many kinds of people, not one staircase to one
summit. Scratch (with the Lifelong Kindergarten group) is the proof: games,
stories, art, simulations, and a community where remixing is a first-class verb.
StarLogo and *Turtles, Termites, and Traffic Jams*: decentralized thinking —
people instinctively assume a leader bird, a head termite; massively-parallel
turtle microworlds teach that order self-organizes. The 4 P's: projects, passion,
peers, play. Kindergarten is the model for all learning, and it's being
squeezed out of kindergarten itself.

## Votes

- **Widen the walls** — many kinds of projects for many kinds of minds; the tool
  that supports only one genre of creation teaches only one kind of kid
  ([../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md](../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md);
  ["Designing Construction Kits for Kids"](https://web.media.mit.edu/~mres/papers/IDC-2005.pdf))
- **Make remixing a first-class verb** — Scratch's See Inside button is
  [tourist-policy](../methods/tourist-policy.md) for code; every project is an
  open door
- **Teach decentralized thinking** — no leader bird, no head termite; run the
  massively-parallel microworld until the instinct for central control dies
  (*Turtles, Termites, and Traffic Jams*;
  [../methods/tuned-emergence.md](../methods/tuned-emergence.md))
- **Run on the 4 P's** — projects, passion, peers, play; check any learning
  design against all four (*Lifelong Kindergarten*)
- **Protect kindergarten from school** — the making-first model is being squeezed
  out of the one place that had it; export it upward instead

## Vetoes

- Don't build one staircase and call it a curriculum
- Don't assume a leader where emergence will do
- Don't add ceilings while widening walls — see Snap! for what no ceiling costs and buys
- Don't gamify what play would carry
