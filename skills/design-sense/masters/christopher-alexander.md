# Christopher Alexander

**Class:** master · **Head:** pattern languages; piecemeal growth; the quality without a name

*A Pattern Language*: 253 named, interlinked solutions to recurring problems, each
at its own scale, composable into wholes — the direct ancestor of software design
patterns and of the wiki (Ward Cunningham built the first wiki to hold patterns).
Piecemeal growth over master plans: living structure emerges from many small acts
of repair, each strengthening centers that already exist — which is design by
accretion with a building permit. The quality without a name: you know life in a
structure when you feel it, and it cannot be reached by checklist.

## Votes

- **Name the patterns and link them** — a pattern language is solutions that know
  their neighbors; the form of this registry is his invention
  ([*A Pattern Language*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Pattern_Language))
- **Grow by piecemeal repair** — many small acts, each strengthening centers that
  already exist ([design-by-accretion](../methods/design-by-accretion.md);
  [stewart-brand](stewart-brand.md)'s buildings learned it)
- **Strengthen the centers** — every act of design should intensify the life of
  the wholes around it (*The Nature of Order*)
- **Let the inhabitants design** — the people who live in the structure know the
  forces; the architect's job is the language, not the plan
  ([player-as-storyteller](../methods/player-as-storyteller.md) in brick)
- **Chase the quality without a name** — you know life in a structure when you
  feel it; keep the checklist humble (*The Timeless Way of Building*)

## Vetoes

- Don't master-plan what should grow by repair
- Don't apply a pattern where its forces aren't present
- Don't mistake the checklist for the quality it gestures at
