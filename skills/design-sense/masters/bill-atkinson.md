# Bill Atkinson

**Class:** master · **Head:** programming for the rest of us

HyperCard: the missing link between Kay's Dynabook and the web — cards, stacks,
buttons, and HyperTalk gave non-programmers a medium where making software was as
natural as making a document, a decade before browsers and with two-way authorship
the web never recovered. MacPaint and QuickDraw before that: the feel of direct
graphical manipulation on hardware that had no right to afford it, because speed
is a feature and roundrects are everywhere once Jobs makes you look at a street
sign. The through-line: collapse the distance between using and making.

## Votes

- **Collapse the distance between using and making** — the HyperCard user is one
  menu away from being a HyperCard author
  ([Wikipedia: HyperCard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HyperCard);
  [low-floor-no-ceiling](../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md))
- **Ship the erector set, not the sculpture** — cards, buttons, stacks: parts that
  compose, so users build things you never imagined
  ([bill-budge](bill-budge.md) made the same vote in pinball)
- **Make speed a feature** — QuickDraw's regions made overlapping windows *feel*
  direct; latency is a design material, not an implementation detail
  ([no-spurious-velocity](../lenses/no-spurious-velocity.md) kin)
- **Let the world edit you** — Jobs pointed at a street sign and roundrects
  entered the toolbox; keep the intake valve open
  ([folklore.org: Round Rects Are Everywhere](https://www.folklore.org/StoryView.py?story=Round_Rects_Are_Everywhere.txt))
- **Count negative lines of code** — reporting "-2000 lines" was the honest
  metric; deletion is progress
  ([folklore.org: -2000 Lines of Code](https://www.folklore.org/StoryView.py?story=Negative_2000_Lines_Of_Code.txt);
  [worse-is-better](../methods/worse-is-better.md)'s "can you remove more?")
- **Give it away to the rest of us** — HyperCard shipped free with every Mac
  because a medium isn't a medium until everyone has it
  ([tourist-policy](../methods/tourist-policy.md))

## Vetoes

- Don't gate creation behind a priesthood
- Don't accept "the hardware can't" before the algorithm has suffered
- Don't ship a medium people can consume but not author
