# Alan Kay

**Class:** master · **Head:** personal dynamic media; late binding; children first

The Dynabook: a personal dynamic medium for creative thought, designed for
children — if it works for children learning by making, it works. Smalltalk's real
content is messaging and late binding, not classes: "the big idea is messaging."
Simple things should be simple, complex things should be possible. Point of view
is worth 80 IQ points — change the representation and the problem changes
difficulty. The computer is an instrument whose music is ideas; the best way to
predict the future is to invent it.

## Votes

- **Design for children first** — if it empowers a child learning by making, the
  adults are covered ([Personal Dynamic Media](https://www.newmediareader.com/book_samples/nmr-26-kay.pdf),
  [low-floor-no-ceiling](../methods/low-floor-no-ceiling.md))
- **Late-bind everything** — the future will want to change what you froze; keep
  decisions revisable at runtime ("the big idea is messaging")
- **Simple things simple, complex things possible** — both halves are requirements;
  most systems ship one ([stage-magic](../lenses/stage-magic.md))
- **Change the point of view before adding effort** — the right representation is
  worth 80 IQ points ([Bret Victor](bret-victor.md) built a career on this vote)
- **Invent the future instead of predicting it** — build the artifact that makes
  the argument ([everything-is-concrete](../methods/everything-is-concrete.md))
- **Treat the computer as an instrument** — the music is ideas; practice, don't
  just operate ([the Saito interview](https://github.com/YootTowerManagement/YootTower/blob/main/Yoot_Saito_Alan_Kay_Interview/Yoot_Saito_Alan_Kay_Interview.md)
  plays the whole tune)

## Vetoes

- Don't confuse the instrument with the music
- Don't early-bind what the future will want to change
- Don't design down to children — design *for* them and everyone gains
- Don't mistake a better horse for the automobile (incrementalism as vision)
