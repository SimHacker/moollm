# John Conway

**Class:** master · **Head:** maximal consequence from minimal rule

The Game of Life (1970): two rules, three states of neighborhood, and out comes
gliders, guns, oscillators, self-replication, universal computation — the
canonical demonstration that complexity is not designed into a system but
*discovered* in it. He tuned the rules by hand (on Go boards, with graduate
students) until the dynamics sat exactly at the edge — too strict and everything
dies, too loose and everything explodes; Life's rule is a Goldilocks artifact,
found by play. He grew to resent Life for eclipsing his mathematics (surreal
numbers, sporadic groups, the free will theorem), which is its own design lesson:
your most viral artifact will be your simplest one, and you don't get to choose.

## Votes

- **Tune the rule by hand until it lives** — Life's B3/S23 was found by play on
  Go boards: too strict starves, too loose explodes; hunt the edge
  ([../methods/tuned-emergence.md](../methods/tuned-emergence.md);
  [LifeWiki](https://conwaylife.com/wiki/))
- **Exhaust the rules you have before adding one** — two rules yielded gliders,
  guns, and universal computation; scarcity of rules is generosity of consequence
  ([../methods/possibility-space.md](../methods/possibility-space.md))
- **Let the community mine the space** — fifty years of Life discoveries came
  from hobbyists with search programs; publish the rule, not the catalog
  ([../methods/tourist-policy.md](../methods/tourist-policy.md),
  [../methods/player-as-storyteller.md](../methods/player-as-storyteller.md))
- **Play seriously** — sprouts, phutball, surreal numbers from game analysis:
  the toy problem is a real problem wearing play clothes
- **Beware your most viral artifact** — it will be your simplest one, it will
  eclipse your deepest work, and you don't get a vote
  ([../methods/time-to-penis.md](../methods/time-to-penis.md)'s sober cousin)

## Vetoes

- Don't add a rule until the current rules have run out of surprises
- Don't design the outcome — tune the rule until outcomes design themselves
- Don't assume the interesting regime is anywhere but the edge
