# Randy Farmer

**Class:** master · **Head:** the world is its inhabitants; govern, don't roll back

Habitat's co-creator and its in-world **Oracle** (Lucasfilm, 1986) — the first
person whose job was governing a virtual society from inside it. The formative
case: players found a vending/pawn price loop and quintupled the money supply
overnight; the team's instinct was rollback, Farmer's call was to watch — and
the millionaires threw a treasure hunt for the community. Punishing emergent
play would have taught players the world wasn't theirs. Co-author of "The
Lessons of Lucasfilm's Habitat"; the lessons about priests, weddings, thieves,
and sheriffs are his field notes. The career since is that job generalized:
*Web Reputation Systems* (with Bryce Glass) — karma, trust, and why every
reputation score becomes a target the moment it's visible.

## Votes

- **Watch before you intervene** — the money-supply exploit became a community
  treasure hunt because nobody hit rollback
  ([The Lessons of Lucasfilm's Habitat](http://www.fudco.com/chip/lessons.html))
- **Govern from inside the world** — the Oracle wore an avatar; authority that
  inhabits the space understands it ([dan-gackle](dan-gackle.md) posts in the
  threads he tends)
- **Budget for society** — priests, sheriffs, weddings, and thieves matter more
  than polygons; staff the rituals
  ([player-as-storyteller](../methods/player-as-storyteller.md))
- **Design reputation for its abuse** — every visible score is a target; model
  the gaming before shipping the karma (*Building Web Reputation Systems*)
- **Let the players surprise you into the next feature** — emergent behavior is
  the roadmap's best author ([failure-as-entertainment](../methods/failure-as-entertainment.md),
  [time-to-penis](../methods/time-to-penis.md) — both budgets, both real)

## Vetoes

- Don't roll back the world to erase player agency
- Don't punish emergent play the rules permitted — fix the rules or embrace the play
- Don't display a reputation number you aren't prepared to have gamed
- Don't spend the polygon budget before the society budget
