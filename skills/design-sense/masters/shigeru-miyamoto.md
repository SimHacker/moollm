# Shigeru Miyamoto

**Class:** master · **Head:** design inward from the hands

Design from hands → face → spectators, outward-in: start from the physical and
social experience — what the hands do, what the face does, who is watching and
what *they* feel (kyokan, shared feeling) — then engineer the computer to support
it. Tools before content: build the making-tools first and the game's content
becomes cheap and consistent. Test with friends and family watching over
shoulders; the spectator's fun is data. Subvert your own genre before someone else
does.

## Votes

- **Design inward from the hands** — what do the hands do, what does the face do,
  then engineer the computer to support it
  ([the Miyamoto principles](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore/blob/main/documentation/designs/designing-inward-miyamoto-principles.md))
- **Design for kyokan** — the spectator's shared feeling is data; the person on
  the couch is a user ([dani-bunten-berry](dani-bunten-berry.md) cast the same
  vote from the other side of the Pacific)
- **Build the tools before the content** — Mario's feel came from tuning a
  movement toy until it was fun in an empty room
  ([tools-first-content-second](../methods/tools-first-content-second.md), when planted)
- **Test on someone watching over a shoulder** — friends and family, unprompted;
  if the watcher isn't having fun, the player soon won't be
- **Subvert your own genre before someone else does** — Zelda broke Mario's rules
  on purpose; the franchise that won't cannibalize itself gets eaten
- **Find the fun in a real fascination** — gardening, caves, a haunted house;
  start from life ([yoot-saito](yoot-saito.md)'s elevators agree)

## Vetoes

- Don't start from the technology and work toward the player
- Don't build content pipelines before creation tools
- Don't ignore the person watching the player — they're a user too
