# Possibility Space

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Will Wright (+ Brian Eno, Long Now dialogue)

> **Design the space, not the path.**

Build open-ended possibility spaces; players supply goals and stories. Toys, not
games: design for replay of the space rather than a single authored path. The
designer's job is the landscape's topology, not the tour route.
