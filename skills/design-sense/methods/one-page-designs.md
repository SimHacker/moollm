# One-Page Designs

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Stone Librande (GDC 2010; Diablo III, SimCity 2013, Riot)

> **The whole system on one dense page.**

Express the entire design as a single annotated poster-style page — a diagram dense
enough to be the spec and legible enough to hang on the wall. The constraint is the
method: if the system won't fit on one page, the design isn't understood yet; when
it does fit, everyone on the team can see the same whole, and the document is too
visible to rot. Iterations are new posters, not new paragraphs — the design stays
a picture of a system instead of a novel about one.

Kin to the semantic mipmap: the one-pager is the design's GLANCE level, forced to
exist first.
