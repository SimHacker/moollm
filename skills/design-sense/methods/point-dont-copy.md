# Point, Don't Copy

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** palmhoo CONSTITUTION Arts. II-III; conscience: Ted Nelson's transclusion

> **One canonical home. Everywhere else points and annotates.**

Every fact gets one canonical home; every other mention points at it and adds only
its local angle. Copying paragraphs breaks edit rights and creates coherence rot —
two copies is one lie waiting to happen. Stubs are topical notes plus links, not
mirrors. The gold standard is Nelson's transclusion: quote by inclusion, provenance
visible, a route back to context always intact.
