# Play, Learn, Lift

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Don Hopkins (after Papert; jazz practice)
**Dispatch:** whole skill at moollm `skills/play-learn-lift/` — load it; this entry is the design-sense pointer.

> **Jazz first, then standards.**

Explore at low stakes, notice which conventions earn their keep, then formalize for
reuse. The lift gate: LIFT is earned — dogfood the concrete instance until it
surprises you, then abstract. Specs-first inverts the order and ships untested
abstractions.
