# Player as Storyteller

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Will Wright; Don Hopkins (SimProv)

> **Authorship lives on the caption layer.**

Creators build systems; storytellers build narrative *with* them. The Sims Family
Album, the Exchange, and SimProv put authorship on the caption layer atop emergence
— play off the emergent behavior, ignore it, retroactively explain it, or direct
every beat. Improv meets playwriting.
