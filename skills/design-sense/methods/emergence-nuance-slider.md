# Emergence-Nuance Slider

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Don Hopkins, reading The Sims storytelling community

> **Emergence vs authorship is a slider, and the player holds it.**

Emergence and authored emotional nuance aren't a binary. Put the slider in the
player's hand: improv one moment ("yes, and" whatever the sim does), playwright the
next (set up and direct every beat), or anywhere between — retroactively explaining
emergent accidents is its own storytelling genre. The engine stays emergence; the
nuance lives in the authorship tools layered on top.
