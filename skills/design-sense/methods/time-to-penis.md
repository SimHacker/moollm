# Time to Penis (TTP)

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** the Spore team at Maxis (industry slang since)

> **Whatever creation tools you ship, players will immediately test them with THAT. Measure it. Plan for it.**

When you give players expressive tools, the first thing a measurable fraction will
express is a penis; TTP is how long it takes. Spore's Creature Creator made the
metric famous; Mythic Quest dramatized it (give players a shovel, get anatomy in
the snow); eBike Safari inherits it out of the box, because GPS route-drawing is
already a folk art form on Strava.

TTP is not (only) a joke — it's a design method with teeth:

- **It's a compliment.** A short TTP proves the tools are expressive and the
  players are engaged. A tool nobody abuses is a tool nobody loves.
- **It's a moderation budget.** TTP tells you when your content pipeline meets its
  first adversarial user — which is launch day, not someday.
- **It's a rhetoric check.** How you *respond* (Spore's ban-hammer vs. quiet
  gallery quarantine vs. leaning in) is procedural rhetoric about who your world
  belongs to.
