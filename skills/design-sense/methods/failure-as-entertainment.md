# Failure as Entertainment

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Will Wright (1996 Winograd talk; GDC Design Plunder)

> **Fail continuously, entertainingly, and legibly.**

Users should fail continuously in an entertaining way *and know why*. Varied,
interesting failure is design fuel; opaque or chaotic failure (SimEarth) kills.
Corollary — **Calvin Syndrome**: players destroy first, to test whether the world is
alive, and then rebuild with empathy. Design the destruction to be as instructive
as the construction.
