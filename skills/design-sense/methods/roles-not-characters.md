# Roles, Not Characters

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Ian Bogost (systems-over-characters essay); Don Hopkins (constructive answer)

> **Give players seats at the table, not mirrors.**

Systems games need roles — mayor, treasurer, planner, advisor — on the player's side
of the glass, not only identifiable protagonists inside it. Roles invite
participation in systems larger than the self; identity-mirroring invites
Balkanization. SimCity simulates no individual characters, yet has many roles to play.
