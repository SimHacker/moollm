# Everything Is Concrete

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** David Ungar & Randall Smith (Self)
**Dispatch:** whole skill at moollm `skills/prototype/` — load it; this entry is the design-sense pointer.

> **Clone a working thing; override the deltas.**

Clone a working prototype and override what differs; don't invent abstract classes
first. Networks of delegation beat taxonomic trees — the abstraction, if it's real,
emerges from the copies rather than preceding them.
