# Design by Accretion

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Chris Trottier (Maxis)

> **Layer, accumulate, let it grow, tune late.**

Build the design up layer by layer, detail by detail — like dust accreting into a
planet — until the whole reaches critical mass; don't top-down specify it. Early
"toilet game" phases are process, not failure. Catalogs lagging the pile are the
signature of the method, not sloppiness. The second half is
[tuned-emergence](tuned-emergence.md); accretion without the tuning pass is just a pile.
