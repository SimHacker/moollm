# Tuned Emergence

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Chris Trottier (2004 Armchair Empire interview; Maxis practice)

> **Fun arrives last. Budget for it.**

The tuning pass is not polish; it is the second half of accretion. Feel and fun
arrive near the end — The Sims wasn't fun until the last months, SimCity 2000 came
together about six weeks before ship. EA's playbook said the late balance pass
shouldn't exist; Maxis needed it. Budget it from the start, and don't judge an
accreting design by its untuned middle.
