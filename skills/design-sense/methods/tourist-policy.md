# Tourist Policy

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** MIT AI Lab (ITS culture, codified in the ITS TOURIST POLICY document)

> **Leave the door open. Tourists become wizards.**

The AI Lab let outsiders — "tourists" — have accounts on ITS to wander, watch, and
tinker, under a published etiquette rather than a lock. No security theater, no
credential gate; the system's openness *was* the recruiting pipeline, and some
tourists became the lab's great hackers. The design method generalizes to any
system, community, or codebase: make the observable surface genuinely explorable,
publish the house rules instead of hiding the house, and budget for the mess —
because legitimate peripheral participation is where contributors come from.

The modern echoes: open source itself, public dev servers, view-source, sandbox
modes, repos readable by strangers and LLMs alike. The anti-pattern is the walled
garden that wonders why nobody grows.
