# Explorable Explanations

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Bret Victor (2011)

> **Turn prose into something you can poke.**

Victor's name for documents whose diagrams and numbers are alive: reactive
documents (the Tangle library) where changing one number re-derives the argument,
and explorable models where the reader plays the system instead of trusting the
author's summary. The reader becomes an active critic of the model rather than a
consumer of its conclusions — "an active reader asks questions, considers
alternatives, questions assumptions."

The design-sense use: whenever an explanation matters, ask what the live version
would be. A spec, a tuning doc, a postmortem — each has an explorable form where
the claims are sliders. Micropolis's plan to animate Gingold's
[reverse diagrams](reverse-diagrams.md) against a live simulator is exactly this
method applied to simulation archaeology.
