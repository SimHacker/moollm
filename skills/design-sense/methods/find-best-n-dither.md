# Find-Best-N Dither

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** Will Wright (Sims autonomy); Don Hopkins

> **Score everything. Then don't take the max.**

Don't take argmax: score the advertisements, then pick randomly among the top N.
Deliberate suboptimality escapes local maxima, honors that scores aren't truth, and
leaves room for the teacher's override to land. Perfectly optimal agents are
unteachable, boring, and wrong with total confidence.
