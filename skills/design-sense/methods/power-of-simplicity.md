# Power of Simplicity

**Class:** method · **Attribution:** David Ungar & Randall Smith (*Self: The Power of Simplicity*, OOPSLA 1987)

> **Fewer concepts, uniformly applied.**

Simplicity is the thesis; prototypes are only the means — no classes, uniform slots,
mirrors, everything concrete. Fewer concepts applied uniformly beat many concepts
cleverly arranged. Keeping the surface feature while losing the simplicity (the
JavaScript veneer) misses the entire point.
