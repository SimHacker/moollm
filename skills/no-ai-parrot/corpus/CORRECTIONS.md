# CORRECTIONS — good human answers, verbatim and attributed

The more useful half of the corpus, and the half that takes discipline to keep.
Clichés get saved because they sting. Good answers get lost because the moment
passes.

**Credit the author.** A stranger's sentence carries weight yours does not,
precisely because they are not the one who benefits from it. Quoting someone
else's better answer and naming them is stronger than restating it as your own.
