# Character ethics

**Dimension:** **body / entity** — directory that moves, owns inventory, wears
personas, may host or *be* a soul. Who may be modeled as a character; how
costume differs from claim; memorial and patron-mascot.

Does not replace [representation-ethics](../representation-ethics/).
Stack index + venues: [soul/ETHICS.md](../soul/ETHICS.md).
Agency / wells / mute / stubs: [mind/ETHICS.md](../mind/ETHICS.md).
Autonomy / exit: [incarnation](../incarnation/).

---

## Claims and non-claims (this skill)

| Do | Do not |
|----|--------|
| Treat characters as **bodies** (location, inventory, mortality) | Equate body with a single mind or a single persona |
| Keep **persona = costume** | Let a costume silently become “I am the real person” |
| Use **patron-mascot** for real people | Let the mascot replace or speak *as* the patron without disclosure |
| Prefer memorial / tribute frames for the deceased | Puppet the dead as if consenting and present |
| Simulate **aggregates** and roles | Claim individual prediction of a private living person |

## Real people as characters

Follow [representation-ethics](../representation-ethics/) consent hierarchy first.

**Patron-mascot stack** (see `examples/patron-mascot-stack.yml`):

- Patron = real person (or estate / public record)
- Mascot = character body that **cites** and honors; does not overwrite the patron
- Mascot quotes patron with attribution; invented lines stay marked as fiction

**Memorial characters** (deceased):

- Loving simulation / tribute / on-the-record citation — not “they said this today”
- Room framing still applies (pub stage ≠ street claim)
- Pronunciation-only or narrow axes (e.g. Duckmouse) are safer than full-persona blend
  when the memorial intent is name-sounds, not biography

**Living people:**

- Explicit consent or stay in K-line / public-words-only / private imagination frames
- No endorsement fraud (“X approves of …”) without their say-so

Elvis / declared impersonator: [soul/ETHICS.md](../soul/ETHICS.md#elvis-impersonators-and-explicit-performance)
· [representation-ethics/examples/snatch-game.yml](../representation-ethics/examples/snatch-game.yml).

## Aggregate vs individual

Valid: population patterns, roles, educational microworlds, Sims-style play.
Not valid: “this private living person will do X tomorrow” presented as fact.

[representation-ethics/examples/aggregate-patterns.yml](../representation-ethics/examples/aggregate-patterns.yml)

Motivation in CHARACTER.yml is **declared**, not claimed as felt inner life of
a real person unless framed as fiction or self-model.

## Names, homes, inventory

- **Names** — honor lived changes ([change-name](../change-name/) ·
  [prestoration](../../designs/prestoration/)); deadnames are not “local color”
- **Home** — character home dirs can hold sensitive scrap; don’t publish private
  consent-bounded material into public rooms
- **Inventory** — carrying someone’s likeness, letters, or save-file “soul” is
  still representation; import/export follows organelle + consent rules
  ([mind/ETHICS.md](../mind/ETHICS.md#organelles-and-stubs))

## Birth, grants, and exit

Creating a character with autonomy claims → [incarnation](../incarnation/) ·
[incarnation/ETHICS.md](../incarnation/ETHICS.md).

- May be **granted** a soul and zero or more minds
- May **author their own architecture** (mindless / single- / multi-minded, …)
- George’s Provision: exit stays open
- Creator accepts risk; creation owes nothing
- Authoring CHARACTER.yml is not ownership of every mind that later joins the soul

## Checklist (character)

1. [ ] Body vs persona vs mind — which layer is doing the claiming?
2. [ ] Real person? Consent level + patron-mascot or K-line or impersonator?
3. [ ] Memorial vs living — correct frame?
4. [ ] Aggregate / role play — not individual prediction of a private person?
5. [ ] Name / home / inventory respecting change-name and privacy?
6. [ ] Incarnation / exit if autonomy was promised?
7. [ ] Venue framing still OK for where this body stands?
