# Vehicle ethics

**Dimension:** **movable inhabitables as moral loci** — a vehicle is a room that
relocates. When it moves, lands, hits, or carries, ethics move with it.

Dorothy’s house was a vehicle (airborne portable room). It landed on a witch
and killed her. Inhabitability does not grant moral immunity.

Does not replace [representation-ethics](../representation-ethics/ETHICS.md).
Venue framing: [room/ETHICS.md](../room/ETHICS.md).
Occupants: [character/ETHICS.md](../character/ETHICS.md) · [soul/ETHICS.md](../soul/ETHICS.md).
Who is driving: [mind/ETHICS.md](../mind/ETHICS.md) (stubs / pilots).

## Claims and non-claims (this skill)

| Do | Do not |
|----|--------|
| Treat DRIVE / land / crash as **acts with consequences** | Pretend “it was just a room relocating” |
| Name **pilot / rider / hitcher / weather** responsibility | Blame only the vehicle when a mind was steering |
| Carry **room framing** into the new world pin | Assume stage license survives a cross-country DRIVE |
| Allow vehicle-as-character (FMC, study) full ethics stack | Exempt vehicles from representation or incarnation rules |
| Document cargo and hitchlings in harm’s way | Stow living beings as silent luggage without consent |

## Dorothy’s house (founding case)

```text
Kansas farmhouse (vehicle) → cyclone (DRIVE without willing pilot)
  → lands in Oz on Wicked Witch of the East → death
Dorothy (occupant) did not intend homicide; the vehicle still killed.
Munchkin framing: liberation narrative ≠ “no ethics applied.”
```

Lessons:

1. **Unpiloted / forced DRIVE** still has victims — weather, malfunction, curse,
   and occupant share a responsibility graph; do not erase the crush.
2. **Landing is an act** — EMBARK/DRIVE/DISEMBARK are not ethically null.
3. **Local framing matters** after arrival — Oz celebrates; Kansas would call
   the coroner. Re-check [room/ETHICS.md](../room/ETHICS.md) at the new pin.
4. **Footwear / loot** (ruby slippers) is inventory ethics after vehicular
   homicide — [inventory](../inventory/) · representation if the dead had claims.

## Responsibility graph

| Role | Typical duty |
|------|----------------|
| **Pilot / driver / rider** | Steer; brake; choose landing; disclose hitch |
| **Occupant (passenger)** | Consent to ride; may share blame if they ordered DRIVE |
| **Hitcher / caravan lead** | Duty of care for nested wagons and their cargo |
| **Vehicle-as-character** | If the coach has a soul/minds, they may counsel or refuse |
| **Remote stub** | Named pilot remains responsible ([mind/ETHICS.md](../mind/ETHICS.md)) |
| **Weather / force majeure** | Narrate; still account for victims and aftermath |

A horse that bolts and a TARDIS that materializes in a crowd are the same
pattern at different tech levels.

## Framing while moving

- Framing **inherits** with the vehicle’s internal rooms until DISEMBARK.
- On DRIVE to a new world location, **external** ethics = that location’s room
  framing — stage props do not authorize crushing people in the street.
- Party members sharing a vehicle share scene consent ([party](../party/)).

---

## Cargo, hitchlings, and living stowaways

- STOW of persons needs consent / incarnation exit still open.
- Hitching a wagon of prisoners is not “just logistics.”
- Bag-of-holding / bakfiets: portable rooms can hide harm — disclose what’s inside
  when ethics demand (representation, memorial, safety).
