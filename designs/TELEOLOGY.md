# Teleology

What "purpose" means, who means what by it, and why the answer decides what kind of
question you are asking about minds.

Occasioned by Haig Shahinian's objection in the comments of Alan Kay's September 2026
Quora answer on AGI — the only comment in that thread attacking Kay's own position
rather than the state of the field, and the one Kay left unanswered. Folded by Quora
down to "wishes Kay good health," which is how the sharpest thing in a thread
disappears:

## The objection

Cybernetics kept teleology — "or teleonomy, if Dennett helps it go down," he allows —
and modern complexity and AI thinking dropped it. Living things are "subjective
unities, not just objective systems." Society of Mind is "still locked in the old
computational paradigm." What AGI will yield is "high-performing zombies,
capitalism's wet dream." What is needed instead is AUM, an authentic unified mind.

Half of that is sharp and mechanistic. Half is a demand for interiority riding in on
the back of the sharp half. Separating them is the whole point of this document.

## Three genres of question

The detective-fiction frame sorts this better than the philosophy literature does.

| Genre | Question | Field | Purpose lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Whodunit** | who is the agent? | phenomenology, the hard problem, AUM | inside, as a subject |
| **Howcatchem** | the culprit is given; the mechanism is the drama | cybernetics, control theory, Society of Mind | in the loop, as a setpoint |
| **Wherecatchem** | where is it, in the coupling? | ecological psychology, situated action, advertisement economies | in the world, distributed |

**Cybernetics is a howcatchem.** The founding move — Rosenblueth, Wiener and Bigelow,
"Behavior, Purpose and Teleology," *Philosophy of Science*, 1943 — was not preserving
purpose as a mystery. It was mechanizing it: purpose is a reference signal plus
negative feedback plus error reduction. They rehabilitated the word by making it
cheap. Given the setpoint, all of the science is in the pursuit.

**Haig's objection is a whodunit.** And you cannot get a whodunit answer out of a
howcatchem method. Minsky's move is that "who" dissolves into agents and the self is
one of the agents' stories, so Society of Mind does not refute the objection — it
**declines** it, as a methodological commitment. Haig is right that it is locked in.
It is locked in on purpose.

**Wherecatchem is the third term, and it is the one MOOLLM already built.** Purpose
located in the coupling rather than the head: Gibson's affordances, Ashby's
environment as part of the regulator. The Sims' advertisement economy answers "where
is the motive" with *in the furniture* — objects broadcast what they afford,
creatures score the broadcasts against their own motives, nobody scripts the result
([advertisement-economy](../skills/design-sense/methods/advertisement-economy.md)).
That is a shipped, inspectable, tens-of-millions-of-copies answer to a question
neither side of the Quora argument was asking.

## Who means what by it

| Who | Purpose means | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Aristotle, the scholastics | real final causes; the future pulls the present | foe in science |
| **Kant** (*Critique of the Power of Judgment*, 1790) | organisms must be **judged as if** purposive — "natural purposes" as a *regulative* principle, never constitutive | friend, as a lens you are required to use and forbidden to believe |
| **Pittendrigh (1958), Mayr** | **teleonomy** — goal-directedness produced by history, no forward causation | friend, fully domesticated |
| **Wiener, Ashby, Powers** | reference signal, error, feedback; purpose as circular causality | friend, mechanized — and *ownership of the setpoint* becomes the live question |
| **Maturana, Varela, Thompson** | autopoiesis: organizational closure; the system's purpose is its own continued organization | friend, and the strongest thing available to Haig |
| Friston, Hohwy | self-evidencing: acting to maintain one's own model | friend, the modern descendant of the above |
| Vitalism, and AUM as stated | irreducible interiority | not a scientific claim |

**Teleonomy versus teleology is the practical answer to friend-or-foe.** Pittendrigh
coined the word in 1958 precisely to keep the useful part and throw the metaphysics
away. If you can say what the setpoint is and what history installed it, you are
doing teleonomy and you are fine.

## Backwards, and upside down

Two failure modes, and both are named in the shape of the question.

**Backwards.** Teleology explains from a future state. Darwin's version explains from
the *past* in a way that merely looks like it comes from the future. Everything a
trained model appears to want is a shadow of gradient descent already completed: its
purpose is entirely in the past tense, which is why probing it for intent finds
nothing doing the wanting *now*.

**Upside down.** Dennett's strange inversion of reasoning — design without a
designer, competence without comprehension. Which means "high-performing zombies" is
Dennett's explanation restated as a grievance. Chalmers supplies the zombie, Dennett
supplies the reason it is unsurprising, and the objection treats the account as the
scandal.

## The part of the objection that lands

Modern AI did not *drop* teleology. It **outsourced** it.

Cybernetics located the setpoint inside the organism. A loss function is a setpoint
installed from outside, at training time, by whoever pays for the GPUs. At run time
the system has no telos of its own — only one that was welded on and now belongs to
somebody else. So "capitalism's wet dream" is not merely an insult; it is
structurally the correct description of a purposive system whose purposes are
property. That is what a zombie is *for*, commercially.

And it is the same observation Jens Mönig makes from the classroom end: there is
nothing in there to explain, and nobody in there to ask.

> There is no way to explain what's going on as soon as you have one hidden layer.

## The argument Haig had available and didn't use

Autopoiesis. Organizational closure gives a *mechanistic* account of a system having
its own setpoint — the purpose is its own continued organization — and self-evidencing
is the contemporary version. A language model has no such loop: no metabolism, no
boundary it maintains, nothing at run time that would be worse off if it stopped.
That is a checkable structural difference, and it makes his point without requiring
anyone to grant him interiority.

He asked for AUM on faith when closure was available on the merits. Which is the
[reasoning-is-not-science](../skills/design-sense/lenses/reasoning-is-not-science.md)
test applied to an objection rather than to a person: there is no mechanism in AUM, no
prediction, and no cost for being wrong, so there is nothing for Kay to grab. Reading
the silence that way is a reading, not a fact — and the other half of it is that the
comment aims a paradigm accusation at a dead friend's book.

## Circular is the mechanism, not the fallacy

Worth stating plainly because the words collide. **Circular reasoning** is a fallacy:
the conclusion smuggled into the premise. **Circular causality** is Wiener's entire
contribution: the loop is how a system holds a course, and it was close to
unthinkable in science until 1943. Suspecting your own logic of going in circles is
good hygiene. Suspecting a *system* of it is usually just noticing that it works.

## What this means for MOOLLM

| Question | Where MOOLLM stands |
|---|---|
| Where is the setpoint? | In the objects. Advertisements are broadcast by things in the world and scored by the receiver — wherecatchem by construction |
| Who owns it? | Whoever wrote the YAML, and it is on disk in git where anyone can read and change it. The outsourcing is not the problem; hiding it is |
| Does anything here have its own telos? | **No.** No closure, no self-maintenance, nothing that would be worse off if it stopped. Honest limit, same as the fixed motive vocabulary The Sims shipped with |
| Does it claim the inside? | No. Society-of-Mind posture: decline the whodunit, say so out loud, and don't sell a howcatchem as a subject |

The last row is the one to keep. The failure mode for a system that portrays
characters is quietly letting a mechanism get described as a mind, which is why
`real-being` and the representation ethics exist and why the house rule is K-line,
not impersonation — invoke the tradition, never claim the identity.

And the missing closure in row three is a **specification** rather than a gap, once the whole
stack is read as what it is: a set of prosthetics, each aimed at a faculty that is unreliable
on its own. A prosthetic needs no telos of its own and should not have one — the setpoint
stays in the person, and eyeglasses have no opinions. Which converts the standing objection
from *is there someone inside* into a question with a mechanical answer: **whose loop is this
closing?** Answerable here only because the motives are in YAML, in git, with an author and a
date, and a purpose can be a coordinate you print rather than a weight you cannot read
([PROSTHETICS.md](PROSTHETICS.md),
[korz purpose-dimension](korz/korz-prime/examples/purpose-dimension.md)).

## Open questions

- An inverter needs intent. Kay's unsolved "automatic inverter" from the 2011 MVC
  correspondence needs to know what a change was *for* in order to run a mapping
  backwards — is that the same hole as the missing setpoint, in a different costume?
- Could a society of interacting elements **grow new motives** without a designer
  adding them? That is the Sims' honest limit, Kahn's "grow it, don't hand-build it,"
  and closure's demand, all pointing at the same missing piece.
- If purpose is in the coupling, what is the smallest system that owns its own
  setpoint? Autopoiesis says the answer is a cell, which is a discouragingly large
  step from a sprite.
