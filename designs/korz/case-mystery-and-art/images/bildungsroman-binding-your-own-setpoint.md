# The failures are mounted too, and at the end the mark changes

**Nothing on this board was thrown away.** The split dovetail at the left is bracketed as squarely,
lit as well and presented as proudly as the flawless joint at the right. That is the plate's first
claim and it is the definition of the form: a Bildungsroman is not a story in which someone overcomes
their mistakes, it is a story in which **the mistakes are the mechanism.** Wilhelm Meister is made of
his errors. So is Pip, so is Jane Eyre, so is Stephen Dedalus. Remove the failures and there is no
protagonist left, only a competent stranger.

Which is why a plate of the finished joint alone would illustrate the wrong thing. Competence is the
*residue*; formation is the board.

## The mark is the bound coordinate

Look at the stamps. **Pieces one through five carry the same worn, shallow triangle-in-circle** — one
punch, rubbed soft from long use, repeated five times. **The sixth carries a different device
entirely**, a star in a square, struck fresh and deep with bright new wood inside the impression.

The same hands cut all six pieces. Only the last one is signed by those hands.

That is not a decorative flourish, it is guild practice: an apprentice's work went out under **his
master's mark**, and he was granted a mark of his own when he was made master. So the coordinate
literally changes hands, and it does so as a small steel punch:

```yaml
early:  {craft: joinery, whose_purpose: the_master}   # competent or not, it is his setpoint
final:  {craft: joinery, whose_purpose: self}         # same craft, newly authored
```

**Coming of age is binding your own purpose coordinate.** The protagonist begins with `whose_purpose`
held by a parent, a guardian, a benefactor, an institution, a church, a class expectation — and the
form ends when they hold it themselves. Which is why these novels read as *becoming a person* rather
than as *learning a fact*: a fact would be a new value on an existing axis, and this is the axis
changing owner.

## And it is the answer to Haig's question, arrived at slowly

The structural form of Haig Shahinian's demand in the Kay thread is not *is someone in there* but
**can a system bind its own purpose coordinate** — which is autopoietic closure, and which is a
yes-or-no question about mechanism
([purpose-dimension.md](../../korz-prime/examples/purpose-dimension.md)).

**The Bildungsroman is the literary form whose entire subject is a yes to that question.** It takes
four hundred pages, it goes through damage, and the closure is the ending. Which is worth noting
precisely because the honest answer for MOOLLM today is *no* — no closure, no self-maintenance,
nothing that would be worse off if it stopped. The form tells you what a yes would have cost.

## Why this plate gets its own register

The other four registers in this pile each hold a **moment**: a survey plate records a state, a
gallery oil records a composition, a lit stage records a configuration. This subject is **duration** —
a coordinate becoming bound over time — and none of them can carry it without turning into a
storyboard, which would break the one-picture-one-concept rule.

An apprentice's trial board is the only artifact I know of that is **time made into a single object.**
It records a formation rather than a state, in one frame, at one glance. And the register chose itself
from the founding text: the archetype is *Wilhelm Meister's **Apprenticeship***, and the protagonist's
name is *Meister*. The guild vocabulary was in the title the whole time.

`mark that spelled something would be a label. Both stamps are abstract geometric punches.`
