# A competent sheet that happens to be impossible

![A 1934 measured survey plate. Three orthographic views of one angular stone mass whose dimensions contradict each other: 2170 in plan, 2360 in elevation, 2110 in section; one edge convex in elevation and concave in section. A classification table with GENUS, ORDER, DIMENSION, MATERIAL and ANGLE all struck out illegibly. A diagonal red stamp reads NO SLOT MATCHES. The title block's DRAWN BY and CHECKED BY fields are blank](cosmic-horror-no-slot-matches.png)

**Plate for:** [*Case study: mystery as dispatch*](../README.md) — the whatdunit row, cosmic horror as
guard failure · **Girders:** [`cosmic-horror-no-slot-matches.yml`](cosmic-horror-no-slot-matches.yml)

**There is no monster in this picture, and there cannot be one.** The moment you draw the creature you
have bound the class, and binding the class is the one thing this genre refuses. So the horror is in
the paperwork.

What the plate shows is a **faithful record that contradicts itself.** Read the numbers: the same mass
is 2170 wide in plan, 2360 in elevation, and 2110 in section. Its height is 1840, or 1650, or 1680, or
1420. One face carries angle callouts of 37 and 61 degrees in one view and 37 and 58 in another. A
single edge is drawn convex in the elevation and concave in the section, and both are labelled as the
same edge.

**That is the whole distinction between laziness and a miss.** A lazy draughtsman leaves the sheet
blank. This one ruled his borders, set his scale bar at 1:50, hatched every face, carried his
dimension arrows to both witnesses, and produced a complete professional drawing — and the completed
drawing is impossible. The instrument worked. The hand was steady. **The world did not fit the form.**

Which is what Lovecraft's adjectives actually are. *Unnameable, indescribable, non-Euclidean* read as
authorial exhaustion if you assume the author was reaching for a word and failing. Read them instead
as **the transcript of a lookup returning nothing** and they become precise, even clinical: a report
that the vocabulary was queried and came back empty. He is not declining to describe the thing. He is
describing what it is like when description misses.

The classification table is the same report in tabular form. Five fields — GENUS, ORDER, DIMENSION,
MATERIAL, ANGLE — every label still printed and legible, every entry struck through in several
cancelled attempts until the scratching is illegible and the paper is worn thin. **The axes are fine.
The axes are exactly the axes you would want.** Nothing in this sea can be bound to them.

## The difference from the locked room, which is why they share a hand

Both plates come out of the same drawing office on purpose, because they are two verdicts from one
instrument and the contrast is the argument:

| | [Locked room](locked-room-missing-axis.md) | Cosmic horror |
|---|---|---|
| Failure | **a missing axis** — one coordinate absent from the list | **no matching coordinate exists** |
| Recoverable | yes; the solution supplies the axis | no; nothing supplies it |
| The genre's pleasure | the axis arriving | the miss itself |
| As a diagnostic | actionable | **correct, and there is no repair** |

A locked room is a bug report you can close. This is a bug report that is accurate, complete, properly
filed, and has no fix — which is a thing working engineers meet and fiction rarely dramatises.

## Nobody signed it

**The title block's DRAWN BY and CHECKED BY fields are empty**, which the prompt did not ask for and
which is better than what it asked for. A 1934 institutional survey sheet with a plate number, a date,
a scale and a stamp, and no name in either signature field. The drawing was made, it was filed, and
**no one would attach themselves to it.**

That is an unattributed observer — the same failure the
[scramble suit](../../../pkd/perky-pat-and-a-scanner-darkly.md) names from the other direction, where
severing the watcher's identity is what severs the mind. Here it arrives as institutional
self-protection: the record exists, the verdict is `NO SLOT MATCHES`, and the accountability line is
blank. A plate that reports an unbindable class and declines to say who reports it.
