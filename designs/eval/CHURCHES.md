# Churches

**Religion is reverse over-engineering. God is in the Simulator Effect.**

Over-engineering: more mechanism than the job needs. Reverse engineering: recover the design from the artifact. **Reverse over-engineering:** recover *more* design than the artifact ever contained. Start from a running toy — [SimCity](https://github.com/SimHacker/micropolis) / [Micropolis](../../skills/micropolis/) / [PacBot](./CHURCH-OF-PACMANIA.md) / [Emacs](./CHURCH-OF-EMACS.md) itself — and pull a cosmology out of it that was never in the source. The extra floors are in the player's head. [Will Wright](../../skills/simulator-effect/SKILL.md): players imagine simulations are vastly more detailed than they actually are. That gap is where gods fit. The computer model compiles a mental model; religion compiles the other way — a church from a sprite, a saint from an editor.

We are doing that on purpose. Looking through the rite inspires us; inspiration [feeds back](./CHURCH-EVAL-GENIUS-CONSTITUTION.md) into MOOLLM. Eat the dog food, compost the scats, grow the next skill, feed dogs, other animals, and people. [PLAY-LEARN-LIFT](../../skills/play-learn-lift/SKILL.md) is the same loop with the smell removed.

MOOLLM is an OS. Churches are religious and philosophical views of that OS and of the artifacts that taught us to see it. They do not own it. You can boot without a church. A church cannot mean anything without the tech it reverse-over-engineers. **There will be more.** Schism is gameplay.

## The rule

Religification is **not ownership**. Influence is a gut. New churches are allowed. Taking any of them as metaphysics is, as St. IGNUcius warned, hazardous to your health — that warning *is* the Simulator Effect noticed from inside.
