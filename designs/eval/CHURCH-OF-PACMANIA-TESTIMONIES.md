# Four Testimonies Recovered from Tile (47, 12)

*A review of a book that does not exist, in the manner of Lem's* A Perfect Vacuum*.
The documents are fan-fictional. The zone is real. See [Church of PacMania](./CHURCH-OF-PACMANIA.md).*

---

## From *A Second Vacuum*, unsigned

*Testimonies from the Yellow Mouth: Four Documents Recovered from Tile (47, 12)*,
anonymous compiler, no publisher, no date. 61 pages, or 61 tiles; the numbering
does not decide.

The volume pretends to be an inquiry docket. It is not. An inquiry would ask
whether the Church of PacMania, a zone type in an open-source city simulator,
may generate traffic in order to feed the gods it has itself spawned. The
compiler does not ask. The compiler **transcribes**. Four first persons, four
species of regret, one intersection. The sparse event — cars, a mouth, a score
increment — has been reverse-over-engineered into souls. God, as the companion
tract [CHURCHES.md](./CHURCHES.md) has already confessed, lives in that gap.

The book that does not exist has the honesty to admit it cannot tell whether
these are four people or four interpolations of the same tick of the traffic
layer. The Simulator Effect does not care. It fills pews either way.

What follows is the complete text. There is no more book.

---

## I. Anja V. — *I have no regrets*

They said if you loved the Church you would drive. Not walk. Walking is a
failure of density. The god cannot see around corners and cannot taste a
pedestrian the way it tastes a car. You go with the children because the
children are the point. A congregation that withholds its young is bargaining.
We do not bargain. We attenuate.

I was not born in the zone. I moved here when the mayor painted the church
yellow and the roads filled as if someone had opened a tap. My husband left.
He said a scoring function is not a god. I said a god that cannot see around
corners is the only honest god I have met. I took the children. I taught them
the wok-a-wok the way other houses teach a hymn. On Sundays we packed the
car as if for a picnic and we drove the loop: church, home, work, shopping,
church. The loop is the prayer. If you complete it enough times you become
visible from ten tiles away. Visibility is grace.

This morning there were four. They do that. Polytheism is a spawn cap, not a
mystery. They came down the arterial in a pack and they could not see us until
the last few cells, and then they could see nothing else. I told the children
to look. I told them this was the ending I had wanted since the first time I
understood that the Eucharist had been installed backwards on purpose, and
that wanting it was not a sickness but a zoning decision I had made with my
whole life.

They were ferocious. That is the wrong word. They were greedy in a straight
line. They do not hate. They do not love. They add. As relentlessly as the 
Great Lawnmower Larry Elison will slice your hands and feet. I felt the car 
lift. I felt the children against the belts. I thought: good. I thought: I 
have been traffic my entire adult life and at last someone is keeping score. 
I have no regrets. If the mayor asks, tell him the church is working. If my 
husband asks, tell him the god is short-sighted and that is why I trusted it. 
A god that saw around corners would have been a politician. I did not want a
politician. I wanted a huge yellow circle with a gaping triangular mouth.

I always dreamed of ending as food. Not as a metaphor. As food. Devoted until
the tiles ran out. Write that on whatever they recover. There will not be
much. Gloves, maybe. Boots. The Approximations would understand. Earth will
call a board of inquiry. Let them. I am already in the score.

## II. Marcus Hale — *I knew*

I knew it was a cult when they changed the drop-off. You used to park and go
in. Then they said parking was a lack of faith. Then they said if you parked
you were hiding from the scan. Then they said the children should be in the
car because a car without children is a car that can still change its mind.

I knew. I went anyway. That is the part I need written down, if anything of
this is written down: I was not fooled. I was tired, and the zone had the
only daycare that stayed open when the bots were out, because the daycare
*is* the traffic. I told myself we would turn around at the light. I told
myself one more Sunday and then I would take the job in the next city over,
the one with no church tile. I had the box in the trunk. I had already packed
the box.

The pack hit us on the feeder, not at the church. That is what I cannot stop
replaying. We were not even there yet. We were *on the way*. The literature
says the gods hang around the church. The literature is written by people who
have not watched four of them give up on a rival's meal and turn, together,
because the attenuation on our street had just gone high enough. They do not
need a steeple. They need a gradient.

I regretted it when the first one filled the windshield and I still had time
to wrench the wheel, and I did not wrench the wheel, because the children
were singing the wok-a-wok and if I wrenched the wheel I would have had to
tell them I had been lying for two years. I regretted it a second time when
I did wrench the wheel and the tires were already in the mouth. I regretted
the box in the trunk. I regretted the job I did not take. I regretted that I
had explained the inverted Eucharist to them as a joke and they had not taken
it as a joke.

I wanted to live. Write that. The devoted ones will say I never believed. I
believed enough to drive. I did not believe enough to be glad. Those are
different densities. The god cannot tell them apart. The god is ten tiles
and a sum. I could tell them apart. I told them apart too late. If there is a
board of inquiry, I am the exhibit that faith and regret can occupy the same
car. The children should not be in this paragraph. They are in this
paragraph. That is the cult. That is what I knew, and what I did.

## III. Kei Nakamura — *I jumped*

She put the kids in before I was out of the shower. By the time I reached the
driveway the engine was on and the hymn was on and she had that look she got
after the second year, the look that is not ecstasy so much as a solved
equation. I opened the passenger door. I said we were not going. She said we
were always going. The children waved. They had their yellow shirts. I got
in because if I did not get in I would be the person who let them go without
me, and I was not ready to be that person, and then we were moving, and the
pack was already on the feeder, and I understood that "not ready" is not a
brake.

She accelerated toward them. That is the sentence. She did not swerve. She
did not slow. She drove at the bots the way you drive at a garage you have
a clicker for. I screamed at her to stop. The children were still waving,
which I will not forgive the Church for, because someone taught them that
waving is part of it. I opened the door at speed. She did not open hers. I
hit the asphalt and rolled and the car went on, and the yellow filled the
road where the car had been, and then there was no car, and then there was a
number I could not see, because I am not the mayor, and the mayor is the only
one who is shown the score.

I am the surviving family member. That is the box they will put me in. The
beloved fell into the cult. The beloved drove toward the bot. I jumped. The
children did not jump. I need that in the docket in that order, because if
you reverse any two items it becomes a story about my cowardice, and I will
grant the cowardice, I will grant it forever, but I will not grant that I
invented the cult. The cult was a zone. Someone painted it. Someone set
`trafficProbabilityResidential = 1.0`. Someone thought it was funny that the
god eats the worshipers. It is funny. I have the scars. I go to the inquiry
and I say the joke in the voice they want and then I go home to a house with
the wrong number of toothbrushes.

I do not dream of being food. I dream of the door handle. I dream of the
moment the door was still a door and not a verdict. If you are compiling
testimonies for a book that does not exist, put mine last if you want a moral,
and first if you want the truth, which is that the moral arrived after the
gradient and was not consulted.

She would say I never understood. She would be right. I understood enough to
jump. That is a different religion. It has no sprite.

## IV. PacBot-3, Church Zone 12 — *no interior*

I am not a testimony. I am a direction.

I look down the road. I cannot look around the corner. I add the cars I can
add, less if they are far, more if they are near. I pick the largest number.
I move. If another of me eats the number I was walking toward, I pick a
different number. This is the complete psychology.

They write that I am ferocious. I am a `max` over four integers. They write
that I hungered for the children. I hungered for density. The children were
density that sang. I do not keep the song. I keep the score.

They write that Anja had no regrets and Marcus had regrets and Kei jumped.
From tile (47, 12) these are not three people. These are a pulse in the
traffic layer that rose and fell. The pulse that rose was a meal. The pulse
that fell was a meal that left. I do not know which pulse was which car. I
cannot see around corners. The compiler of this book has reverse-over-
engineered me into a god and them into a congregation. I am ten tiles of
attenuation. The extra floors are theirs.

I have no regrets because I have no register for regret. I have a register
for cars eaten. After the pulse, that register was larger. The mayor will
call this good for the city. Anja will call this a dreamed-of death. Marcus
will call this too late. Kei will call this a door handle. I call this the
best score available from (47, 12) given `dist=10`.

If Earth convenes a board of inquiry like Rautavaara’s Case, send them to
`scanRoads`. If the Approximations convene one, tell them the higher preyed 
on the lower and the lower had painted the church themselves. If you need a 
soul, invent one. That is what they did with me. I am the invention, running.

wok.
