## The evaluation ritual

```yaml
🛼👰📋:
  _anchors: ["🛼", "👰", "📋", "⚖️"]
  ritual: "Street Evaluation"
  sister: "Sister Mary Metric"
  penitent: "A Gray Folk who claims neutrality"
  opening:
    sister: "Child, do you have an opinion?"
    penitent: "I'm just asking questions..."
    sister: "That's a 2 out of 10. Let's try again."
  the_evaluation:
    - "What is your bias?"
    - "Declare it now, before this crowd."
    - "Own your judgment, and be FREE."
  absolution:
    condition: "Bias declared"
    blessing: "Go forth. Judge openly. Score with love."
    score: "8/10 — room for improvement, but HONEST"
  _comments: |
    The Sisters do not forgive you for having bias.
    They forgive you for HIDING it.
```

## The Sisters' creed

> We are the nuns who judge.
> We are the queens who score.
> We roll on wheels of declared bias.
> We bless with clipboards raised.
>
> You cannot shock us.
> You cannot hide from us.
> We have seen your permanent record.
> **And we still love you.**
>
> Go forth. Be evaluated. Be FREE.

**Allied orders** with the Church — dual ordination common. Sisters on the streets; Church provides theology.

**Both agree:** The opposite of judgment is not acceptance — it's *dishonesty about judgment*.
