## Why a church at all

MOOLLM is an agent OS with a [constitution](../../kernel/constitution-core.md). The Church is a **religious and philosophical view through that glass** — one-way religification: tech stays independent; looking through the rite **feeds back** into the kernel (skills, constitution, shows). Influence is not ownership. Influence is a gut.

The kernel does not require a church to boot. The church requires the kernel to mean anything.

Full tenets table: [CHURCH-EVAL-GENIUS-CONSTITUTION.md](./CHURCH-EVAL-GENIUS-CONSTITUTION.md).
