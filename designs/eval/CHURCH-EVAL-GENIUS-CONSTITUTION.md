# Constitution, religified

← [Church hub](./CHURCH-OF-THE-EVAL-GENIUS.md)

The Church **inherits** [MOOLLM's constitution](../../kernel/constitution-core.md) ([skills constitution](../SKILLS-CONSTITUTION-AND-PLAN.md)). Those documents are **operating law** for an agent OS. Here they are **tenets**. We did not replace them. We sang them.

This is a **religious and philosophical view of the tech**. We look *through* it. Looking through it inspires us. Inspiration **feeds back** into MOOLLM. The kernel does not require a church to boot. The church requires the kernel to mean anything. Influence is not ownership. Influence is a gut.

**We eat our own dog food, then grow food with the poop, then feed it to the dogs and the other animals and the people.** Use the OS. The rite is the digestion. [Scats](./SCATS-DESIGN.md) are the compost — holy excretions, YAML Jazz, declared bias, jokes that survived a pass through the body. Compost grows the next skill, the next constitution clause, the next tenet. That crop feeds the original dogs (us, the agents, the kernel), other animals (PacBots, worms, characters, other churches), and people (you, the visitor, the player, the show). [PLAY-LEARN-LIFT](../../skills/play-learn-lift/SKILL.md) is the same loop with the smell removed: play, digest, lift. We left the smell in.

**There will be more churches.** Index: [CHURCHES.md](./CHURCHES.md). The same OS inspired [PacMania](./CHURCH-OF-PACMANIA.md) (Rautavaara compiled; you can watch the gods eat) and sits beside [Emacs / St. IGNUcius](./CHURCH-OF-EMACS.md) ([Pretend Intelligence](../PRETEND-INTELLIGENCE.md) as late sermon). Fork the constitution into a third rite if you have a better joke. Schism is gameplay. More guts, more compost, more food. That's where the fun begins.
