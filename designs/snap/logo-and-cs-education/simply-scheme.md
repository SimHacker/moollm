# Simply Scheme — Brian Harvey & Matthew Wright

Foreword by Hal Abelson.

**Read it free:** [full text online](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~bh/ss-toc.html) at Brian's
Berkeley page.

## The hook

Scheme without the intimidation factor — same intellectual honesty as SICP, warmer front door.
Brian's lifelong bet: you don't filter people out of computer science; you invite them in with
joy and rigor at once. Designed as a prequel to *Structure and Interpretation of Computer
Programs* — in Brian's words at the [Karlstrom address](../snapcon-2025/karlstrom-address-brian.md),
"still the best computer science course ever invented."

## Show hooks

- Teaching CS 61A / SICP — what Brian kept and what he translated for newcomers.
- *Simply Scheme* as the readable spine behind BJC's "beauty and joy" slogan.
- Hal Abelson's foreword — the MIT lineage Brian carried to Berkeley.
