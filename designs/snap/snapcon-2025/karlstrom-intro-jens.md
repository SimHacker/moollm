## Jens's introduction

Jens opened the post-dinner address as Brian's friend and collaborator. He did not need to
introduce him — the room knew Brian — but framed the Karlstrom award as recognition of Logo books,
Snap!, and a life of **Promethean** teaching (fire from the gods to humans).

### Birthright line

Jens recalled Brian at MIT Sloan (Juke in the room) saying **"computing is your birthright as a
human being"** — goosebumps material that stuck for years.

### The ask to Brian

Radical, non-conformist, surprising, maybe mundane visions about education, computing, AI, and the
state of CS education — from "the world's best programming teacher," now an official thought leader.

## Q&A — Jens moments

### Dutch teacher parallel

- Math teacher since 1988; CS teacher from 2017.
- 1970s Dutch equivalent of Lincoln-Sudbury — slept at teachers' houses, ate with them, went on holiday together.
- First year teaching CS (2017–18) was hard; kids expected C++ because games are written in C++; Jens/Snap! path instead.
- Wanted Barcelona STEAM conference trip; **21st century forbid it**. *"Thank you for celebrating the 20th century."*

### YKCA on "my friend"

Asked about Brian naming every CCUS member "my friend" — deeper message about institutions making
teachers enemies of kids; grades vs holistic relationship (Martin Buber).
