## The hook

Snap! is Berkeley pedagogy in the browser — but it also rode **enterprise research patience**:
Jens as SAP researcher, Alan Kay's **Communications Design Group** funded by SAP (~2013, Vishal
Sikka era), distributed collaboration (Walldorf, Barcelona, California) with Brian Harvey's BJC team.
