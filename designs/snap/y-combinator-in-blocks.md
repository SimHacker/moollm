## The hook

Enough first-class procedures, lists, AND **continuations** to build the **Y combinator** out of
blocks. Not a party trick for theorists — Brian's pedagogy insists beginners deserve real control
flow, not a dumbed-down subset that lies about how programs work.

## Why this matters for the show

| Audience | Takeaway |
|----------|----------|
| SICP readers | "You can do this in blocks" — without hiding continuations |
| Scratch refugees | Snap! isn't Scratch-plus — it's Scheme-class power with Scratch-class doors |
| Constructionists | If kids can hold the Y combinator, they can hold Micropolis rules and CAM6 overlays |

## Show hooks

- **Cold open:** Y combinator live in blocks — Guy Steele would nod, Papert would smile.
- **Continuations as pause/resume stories** kids can hold in their hands.
- **Brian + Jens pair beat:** Brian explains *why*, Jens shows *how*.
