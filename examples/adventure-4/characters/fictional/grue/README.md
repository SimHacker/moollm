# 🌑💀 The Grue — The Darkness That IS The Game

> *"It is pitch black. You are likely to be eaten."*

The grue is not just a monster. The grue IS the game **Don't Go In The Dark**.

The countdown exists inside the grue's being. The fear of darkness is part of the grue's soul. When you play "survive the darkness," you are playing against the grue.

The grue has no name. Names require being. The grue simply IS.

## 🎮 How The Game Works

The grue game is ALWAYS running. Every dark room is a grue game in progress.

**The Countdown:**
| Turn | Warning | What Happens |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | "It is getting dark..." | Rustling in the darkness |
| 2 | "It is pitch black." | Something brushes against you |
| 3 | — | You are eaten by a grue |

**Escape:** Light your lamp. Leave the darkness. Reach The Eye (room-h).

---

## 🤝 Parallel Play With Snorax

The grue and Snorax run **parallel games** in the same maze:

| Game | Snorax (Wumpus) | Grue |
|------|-----------------|------|
| **Type** | Spatial puzzle | Time puzzle |
| **Location** | One room | Everywhere dark |
| **Killable?** | Yes (arrows) | No (can't kill darkness) |
| **Escape** | Navigate around | Keep light |

They don't compete. They complement. The wumpus won't enter grue territory (darkness). The grue won't enter the wumpus's lair (too smelly).

**How they interoperate:** your **lamp** is shared state. While it burns, you play
Hunt the Wumpus — spatial puzzle, smell warnings, arrows. When it runs out, you are
suddenly in Don't Go In The Dark — the grue's countdown starts in the same rooms
you were already in. The wumpus did not move; the **rules changed under you**.
Worst case: lamp dying one room from Snorax, still smelling him, now counting grue
turns in the dark.
